Privacy Policy for LexiDuel

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game LexiDuel (Package Name: com.kakagkai.lexiduel), developed by kakagkai studio.

1. Overview and Local Data Storage

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. LexiDuel is designed to operate primarily offline and on-device:

  • Your gameplay progress, statistics, guessed words, sound preferences, cosmetic tile skins, and in-game coins are stored solely on your local device.
  • We do not require you to create an account, register personal credentials, or provide sensitive personal information to enjoy the game.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

To provide LexiDuel as a free-to-play title, we use third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC):

  • Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AAID), IP addresses, and interaction logs with ads to serve, measure, and optimize advertising.
  • For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK) to obtain explicit consent for personalized or non-personalized advertisements in compliance with GDPR.
  • To review how Google handles collected information, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

In the event of technical crashes or performance issues, anonymous crash diagnostics may be transmitted to help us fix bugs and improve stability across diverse Android hardware.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

LexiDuel is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe that a child has inadvertently provided personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove such information.

5. User Rights and Data Control

Under applicable data privacy laws (such as GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Revoke or update your advertising consent at any time via the Privacy Options in the in-game Settings menu.
  • Reset your Android Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings -> Google -> Ads).
  • Delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing application storage or uninstalling the game.
