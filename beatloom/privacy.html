Privacy policy

Beatloom for Android · effective 1 October 2026

English · Українська · Polski

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Beatloom, the music studio app for Android (package io.github.kakagkai.beatloom). Beatloom is made by an individual developer, kakagkai, based in Wrocław, Poland, who is the data controller for the app.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We don't run servers that receive your data.
  • Your projects, recordings, settings and exports stay on your device.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking of our own.
  • The free version can show a video ad, but only when you tap “Watch a short video”. That ad comes from Google AdMob, which processes some data as described below.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Data that stays on your device

Projects, audio you import or record, autosaves, backups, presets and app settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Files you export (for example MP3 or WAV) are saved to the Documents/Beatloom folder on your device. Nothing of this is uploaded anywhere by Beatloom. It leaves your device only if you share a file yourself (for example with the Share button) or if your phone's own Android backup includes app data.

Microphone

Beatloom asks for microphone access only when you start recording or use a feature that listens to an audio input. The audio is processed on the device and saved only into your project. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Ads (Google AdMob)

If you don't have Beatloom Pro, you can unlock one Pro feature for 24 hours by choosing to watch a short video ad (at most three a day). Ads are never shown during playback, during recording or at startup.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and UK). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google acts as an independent controller of that data.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Beatloom shows Google's consent form before ads can be requested, so you can choose personalised or non-personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Beatloom Pro → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Beatloom Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase belongs to your Google account (a purchase token and order ID), and keeps a note on your device that Pro is unlocked. We don't receive your name, email address or payment card details.

Internet access

Beatloom works offline. It uses the internet only for ads you choose to watch, the ad consent form, and Google Play purchases.

Children

Beatloom is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data stays on your device, you are in control of it: delete projects inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Files you exported to Documents/Beatloom stay there until you delete them. For data processed by Google for ads, see Google's privacy policy and your ad settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). Since we don't hold personal data about you on our side, most requests can be handled on your device as described above, but you can always contact us.
