Beatloom

A music studio that fits in your pocket

Coming soon to Google Play.

Beatloom is a full music studio: a step sequencer, a piano roll, a song playlist, a mixer with effects, synths and real recorded instruments. Make a beat on the bus, finish the song at home.

What's inside

  • Channel rack and step sequencer, piano roll, playlist with audio clips and automation
  • A mixer with inserts, sends and a stack of effects
  • Synths, drum kits and recorded pianos, strings, guitars, brass and more
  • Record from the microphone, export MP3 for free
  • Beatloom Pro (one-time purchase, or 24 hours for a short video): WAV/FLAC, stems, video export, recorded instruments in exports, FL Studio project import, MIDI controllers
  • English, Ukrainian and Russian interface

Privacy

No account, no tracking by us: your projects stay on your device. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. Read the full privacy policy (українською, po polsku).
